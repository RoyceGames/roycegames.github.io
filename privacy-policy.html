03

Use, sharing, and recipients

The partners named below may process advertising information to display contextual or, where allowed, personalized ads; measure impressions and interactions; limit repetition; attribute installs; and detect fraud. RoyceGames may process information that a person voluntarily sends in a support or privacy request. Advertising information may be disclosed to ad networks, mediation providers, bidding partners, and their service providers. Website hosting may involve technical request data. Support information may be shared with service providers or authorities when required by law. Some partners act as independent controllers/businesses under their own notices; others may process data on our behalf. The exact role must be checked in the applicable contract and SDK configuration.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising disclosures may nevertheless count as a “sale” or “sharing” under California law when they support cross-context behavioral advertising. We will honor applicable opt-out rights and explain the actual practice after the configuration audit.

05

EEA and UK privacy rights

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis depends on the activity. We expect to rely on consent for personalized advertising and access to device information where law requires it; legitimate interests, subject to a balancing assessment, for appropriate security, fraud prevention, and responding to voluntary support requests; and legal obligation for mandatory compliance. The final purpose-by-purpose basis and any consent-exempt contextual advertising must match the production implementation. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting processing before withdrawal.

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. You may object to direct marketing and withdraw consent. You may also complain to a competent data protection supervisory authority. If automated ad profiling is used, we will explain meaningful information about it and any applicable safeguards; we do not intend to use it to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Ad providers and hosting services may process information outside the EEA or UK. Where required, international transfers must be supported by an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, the UK addendum, or equivalent lawful safeguards. Specific transfer locations and safeguards must be checked with each provider before publication.

U.S. state opt-out signals, including supported Global Privacy Control signals, must be honored where applicable.
