RG / YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

Control and requests

Your
Choices.

This page explains how players can request access, deletion, correction, or an advertising opt-out. Its contact address and app controls still need to be completed and tested before publication.

No live web switch is available

A click on this website cannot change the settings inside a game. We will only describe an in-app Privacy Settings control here after it exists and is verified to pass choices to every relevant advertising partner.

01 / APP

Change advertising consent

For EEA, UK, and Swiss players, the planned consent interface must offer a way to accept, refuse, or withdraw applicable advertising consent and revisit that decision later. The in-app entry point is [IN_APP_PRIVACY_SETTINGS_PATH]. Until it is implemented, this text does not indicate that such a control exists.

02 / CALIFORNIA

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

If advertising disclosures constitute a sale or sharing under California law, residents must be able to opt out. The verified request method will be [US_STATE_OPT_OUT_METHOD]. A monitored privacy email can also receive requests once privacy@example.com is replaced with a working address. We must connect the choice to the games and partners before this page can serve as a functional opt-out route.

03 / RIGHTS

Access, correct, delete, or limit

To exercise applicable GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or CPRA rights after publication, use the monitored address that will replace privacy@example.com. Include the game name, platform, country or state, and the right you wish to exercise. This example.com address is currently inactive. An authorized agent may contact us where permitted. We will ask only for information reasonably needed to find or verify the relevant record and respond within the applicable time limit. Do not send a government ID or advertising identifier with the first message.

04 / DEVICE

Use device controls

Our iOS games are planned to show an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission prompt before tracking that requires it; the final build must confirm the timing and behavior. On iOS, review Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking for ATT choices. On Android, use your device's current advertising privacy controls to reset or delete its advertising ID where available. These settings can limit some tracking, but they do not replace a request to RoyceGames or a choice inside the app.

05 / PARTNERS

Visit partner choices

Some partners provide their own controls, including Liftoff and Mintegral. Their controls are separate from RoyceGames' responsibilities. See the partner list for their notices.

06 / SIGNALS

Global Privacy Control

Where applicable, qualifying opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control must be honored. This static website contains no ad or analytics script, but an in-app SDK does not automatically receive a browser signal. The implementation must document how relevant signals and state-specific choices reach each partner.
